<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Client → Google backend | Cookies and auth headers cross an untrusted network; must be redacted in any persisted artifacts. |
| SDK → local filesystem (HAR/report) | HAR files and probe reports are written to user-specified paths; must not contain recoverable secrets. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12-01 | Information Disclosure | src/har.rs | high | mitigate | Redact cookie values, Authorization, and x-goog-ext headers; scan POST body for cookie-like substrings before writing. |
| T-12-02 | Denial of Service | src/retry.rs | medium | mitigate | Cap retries (max 3, 8 s max interval, 30 s elapsed); do not classify all 400s as transient, only the documented WIZ pattern. |
| T-12-03 | Spoofing | src/session.rs | medium | mitigate | NotSignedIn is derived only from absent signed-in markers or explicit cookie rejection by /app; do not infer from unrelated substrings. |
| T-12-04 | Tampering | Cargo.toml / dependencies | low | accept | No new dependencies introduced; existing crate versions remain pinned. |
</threat_model>
